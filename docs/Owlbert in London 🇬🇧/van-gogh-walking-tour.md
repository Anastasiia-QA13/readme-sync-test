Van Gogh Walking Tour: In the Footsteps of a Master
Embark on a captivating journey through the landscapes that inspired Vincent van Gogh, one of the world's most celebrated and influential artists. A Van Gogh Walking Tour offers a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in the very scenes that he immortalized on canvas, from the picturesque streets of Arles to the tranquil fields of Auvers-sur-Oise.
These self-guided or guided tours take you to significant locations where Van Gogh lived, worked, and found inspiration. In the charming town of Arles in the South of France, for example, you can trace his steps to see the very spots where he painted masterpieces like Café Terrace at Night and Starry Night Over the Rhône. You will discover the yellow house where he hoped to establish a community of artists and the hospital gardens where he sought treatment. Bronze markers often guide the way, making it easy to follow the trail.
For a deeper dive into his final days, a tour in Auvers-sur-Oise, a quiet village near Paris, is a must. Here, you can walk through the wheat fields that feature in his last paintings and visit the room at the Auberge Ravoux where he spent his final moments. The local church, immortalized in his art, is also a key stop.
Beyond France, you can also follow Van Gogh's early life in his native Netherlands. In towns like Zundert and Nuenen, routes pass through the countryside that shaped his early work, including the famous luminous bicycle path in Nuenen that celebrates his work.
