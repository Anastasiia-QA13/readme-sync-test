The History of Big Ben
Design and construction: The Elizabeth Tower was designed by Augustus Pugin as part of Charles Barry's design for the new Palace of Westminster after a fire in 1834. The clock mechanism was designed by Edmund Beckett Denison.
The bell: The 13-ton Great Bell was originally cast by George Mears, but cracked soon after casting. After this, a lighter alloy was used for the hands of the clock. The bell was likely named after Sir Benjamin Hall, who oversaw the installation.
The name: The tower was originally named the Clock Tower, but was renamed the Elizabeth Tower in 2012 to celebrate the Queen's Diamond Jubilee.
