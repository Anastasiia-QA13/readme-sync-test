Historic Harlem Listen & Walk!
This self-guided audio tour, available through apps like VoiceMap and Free Tours by Foot, offers a deep dive into the history and culture of one of New York City's most iconic neighborhoods. It's a "Listen & Walk" experience that allows visitors to explore at their own pace, following a GPS-enabled map while listening to researched commentary from local experts.
The tour introduces you to the neighborhood where jazz took flight and the Harlem Renaissance redefined American culture. The narrative covers a span of centuries, from Harlem's early Dutch roots to its pivotal role in the Civil Rights Movement. You can walk in the footsteps of legends like Langston Hughes, Duke Ellington, and Ella Fitzgerald,  visiting important sites and landmarks.
