"Text" refers to any written or symbolic material that conveys meaning, from traditional books and articles to digital content, and can be categorized by purpose (e.g., narrative, expository, argumentative). Analyzing text involves various strategies, such as making connections between different texts or understanding a text's context, structure, and features. For English language learners and students, improving writing involves practices like reading widely, using a thesaurus, and understanding common collocations.
