The Hollywood Walk of Fame is an iconic landmark in Los Angeles, located along Hollywood Boulevard and Vine Street. Established in 1958, it is a public sidewalk featuring over 2,500 brass stars that honor entertainers for their contributions to the film, television, music, radio, and theater industries. Tourists from around the world visit to see the names of their favorite actors, musicians, and fictional characters and take photos with the stars. Each year, new stars are added to the pathway to recognize new talent and celebrate legends. The Walk of Fame is a symbol of Hollywood's glamour and success, and it is a must-see destination for anyone visiting Los Angeles.

Each star recognizes an individual's or group's contribution to one of five categories of the entertainment industry:
Motion Pictures: For achievements in film.
Television: For contributions to television.
Music: For achievements in recording.
Radio: For work in the radio industry.
Live Theatre/Performance: For contributions to live stage and theater.
