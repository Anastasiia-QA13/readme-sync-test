A "dinner and a movie" text version is a type of scam where a scammer sends a text message to a random number, pretending to be someone else, to start a conversation and gain trust. These messages often use phrases like "Hi, how are you?" or "Do you have plans today?" and are designed to get a response, confirming the number is active and potentially leading to a request for personal information or money.
How the scam works
The bait: A simple, friendly text is sent to a number, often with a generic greeting.
The hook: The goal is to get you to reply to confirm the number is active.
The manipulation: Once you reply, the scammer may engage in a conversation, build rapport, and eventually use the established relationship to manipulate you into giving them information or money.
Why you shouldn't engage
Confirming the number: Replying confirms to the scammer that your number is active, making you a more attractive target for future scams.
Building a false relationship: The scammer is not a legitimate person; they are using a fake conversation to deceive you.
Protecting your information: By engaging, you risk being manipulated into divulging sensitive information like passwords, account numbers, or other personal data.
