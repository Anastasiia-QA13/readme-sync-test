## Looking for a fun kid-friendly adventure?

This half-day, interactive scavenger hunt led by Owlbert will take you around California's East Bay, exploring stunning destinations such as the Oakland Rose Garden, Lake Merritt, and the Berkeley Marina :boat:

This one's only available during the Spring & Summer!
