# GGB Audio Tour for Android

The Golden Gate Bridge (GGB) Audio Tour for Android provides an immersive, self-guided experience for exploring one of the world's most iconic landmarks. This documentation covers the key features, functionality, and user experience of Android-based audio tour applications for the Golden Gate Bridge.

## Overview

The GGB Audio Tour transforms your Android device into a personal tour guide, offering rich audio content about the Golden Gate Bridge's history, construction, and surrounding landmarks. Using GPS technology, the app provides location-based audio that plays automatically as you explore.

## Key Features

### 🎯 GPS-Based Audio Playback

* **Automatic Triggering**: Audio content plays automatically when you reach specific points of interest
* **Location Awareness**: Uses your device's GPS to detect your position and direction
* **Smart Navigation**: Provides audio cues and directions to guide you along the optimal route

### 📱 Android Compatibility

* **Android 11 Support**: Fully compatible with modern Android devices
* **Device Requirements**: Works on both smartphones and tablets with GPS capabilities
* **Performance Optimized**: Designed to run smoothly on various Android hardware configurations

### 🗺️ Interactive Mapping

* **Offline Maps**: Download maps ahead of time to avoid data usage during the tour
* **Real-time Location**: Shows your current position on the bridge and surrounding areas
* **Route Guidance**: Visual indicators help you stay on the recommended path

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

<Accordion title="GPS Not Working" icon="exclamation-triangle">
  **Symptoms**: Audio not triggering at locations, incorrect position on map
  **Solutions**:

  * Ensure location services are enabled for the app
  * Check that GPS is turned on in device settings
  * Wait for stronger GPS signal in open areas
  * Restart the app if position seems inaccurate
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Audio Playback Problems" icon="volume-mute">
  **Symptoms**: No sound, choppy audio, or incorrect audio timing
  **Solutions**:

  * Check device volume and app volume settings
  * Ensure headphones are properly connected
  * Close other audio apps that might interfere
  * Re-download audio content if files are corrupted
</Accordion>
