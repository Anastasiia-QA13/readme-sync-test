Owls are fascinating birds of prey, known for their distinctive appearance and nocturnal habits. Their large, forward-facing eyes provide excellent depth perception, though they cannot move their eyes within their sockets. Instead, owls are capable of rotating their necks up to 270 degrees. Equipped with highly sensitive ears and soft, sound-dampening feathers, they are silent hunters, primarily feeding on rodents, insects, and other small animals. With over 200 species found globally, owls play a crucial role in maintaining ecosystem balance.
